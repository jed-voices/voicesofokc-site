Doing the Next Right Thing with Baylor Hurst

VOICES of OKC Wed, 04 Nov 2026 10:00:00 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

A book began as letters mailed home. Baylor Hurst joins Jed Chappell to talk about the father who saved those pages, the discipline of writing, and doing the next right thing when the whole path is not clear.

The Shawnee author shares how military basic training became the starting point for his novel, The Son of Dust. He and Jed explore ordinary courage, found family, keeping small promises, and the relationships that help creative work move forward. Baylor also reflects on hope and the value of community in Oklahoma City.
