What Survives the Fire | Sabrina Smith on Loss, Hope, and Building 1155

VOICES of OKC Wed, 12 Aug 2026 06:37:48 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

An apartment fire can take a home in minutes. Recovery takes much longer.

Sabrina Smith joins Jed Chappell to talk about losing her home, business equipment, and irreplaceable family memories in a devastating apartment fire. With her daughters depending on her, Sabrina moved into crisis mode before she had time to process what had happened.

She shares the practical and psychological realities that can follow a disaster, including the gap between immediate emergency assistance and the long work of rebuilding a stable life. That experience became part of the vision for 1155, an effort Sabrina is developing to provide temporary housing and basic necessities for people displaced by fire.

The conversation also moves through Sabrina's Georgia upbringing, her creative work with Crystal Pink Events, the relationship between style and dignity, her connection to Oklahoma, and the hope she found in the middle of loss.

Content note: This episode includes non-graphic discussion of an apartment fire, displacement, loss, and the emotional aftermath of disaster.

ABOUT SABRINA

Sabrina Smith is an event planner and designer with a background in interior design. She is the creative force behind Crystal Pink Events and is developing 1155, a vision for temporary housing and practical support for people displaced by fire.
