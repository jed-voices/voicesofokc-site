Mayor David Holt on Leadership, Youth, and the Future of Oklahoma City | Voices of OKC

In this episode of Voices of OKC, student host Jordan Arnett interviews Oklahoma City Mayor David Holt inside City Hall for an honest conversation about leadership, civic responsibility, and investing in the next generation.

Mayor Holt discusses:

  • His journey from Putnam City North to the White House

  • Winning a third term with 86.5% of the vote

  • Leading Oklahoma City through COVID

  • The decision to build a new arena to keep the Thunder

  • Why real change takes patience and persistence

  • How MAPS is investing in youth centers across the city

  • Why he replaced portraits of former mayors with photos of Oklahoma City kids

This episode is a masterclass in thoughtful leadership and a reminder that Oklahoma City's future belongs to those willing to show up, stay engaged, and think long-term.
