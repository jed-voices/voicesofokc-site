Episode 16 | Derrick Sier

There is a difference between living in a community and being formed by one.

In this episode, Derrick Sier joins Voices of OKC to discuss the power of present fathers, the importance of positive adults outside the home, and the responsibility our city has in shaping young lives.

Raised in North Tulsa, Derrick shares how his father didn't just show up — he intentionally surrounded him with other men who reinforced his identity and confidence.

We explore:

• The nuance behind fatherhood in Black communities
• Research on why multiple adult mentors matter
• How community ecosystems change outcomes
• The role Restore OKC plays in workforce exposure
• Why hope is intrinsic before it is external

This conversation reflects the heartbeat of Oklahoma City — relationships that lead to restoration.
