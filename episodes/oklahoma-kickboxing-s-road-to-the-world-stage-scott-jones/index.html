Oklahoma Kickboxing's Road to the World Stage | Scott Jones

VOICES of OKC Wed, 09 Sep 2026 09:10:07 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Scott Jones, CEO of Husky Fat Promotions, is building a path for Oklahoma kickboxing from local gyms toward the international stage. He talks with Jed about turning a memorial show into a growing fight promotion, the discipline that separates trained fighters from bravado, and the pressure of building something ambitious with a small team.

What this conversation explores:

- The origin and growth of Husky Fat Promotions
- Oklahoma's opportunity in kickboxing and striking sports
- The structure of an eight-person, one-night tournament
- Fear, control, and the mental side of fighting
- Parenting, education, and letting children choose their own path

Guest bio:

Scott Jones is CEO of Husky Fat Promotions, an Oklahoma City-based organization producing professional and amateur kickboxing events. K-1 identifies Husky Fat as a U.S. partner working to create opportunities for American fighters, while Husky Fat's Oklahoma events have connected local athletes with national and international competition.
