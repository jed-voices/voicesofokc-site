More Than Accounting: Daniel Haynes on Purpose, Community, and Oklahoma City

VOICES of OKC Daniel Haynes · Founder of Skyward Financial Wed, 27 May 2026 08:04:20 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

There’s a line Daniel Haynes says in this conversation that quietly captures the entire heart of the episode:

“When something is led by love, no one gets left behind.”

Daniel is the founder of Skyward Financial Solutions, a Certified Fraud Examiner, and co-founder of Live Full alongside his wife, Raven. But more than titles, Daniel is someone deeply committed to stewardship, integrity, family, and serving people well.

In this episode, Daniel shares the story of growing up in rural Oklahoma, collapsing during a basketball game because of an enlarged heart, losing a close friend, and eventually discovering accounting as a calling at just 16 years old.

Jed and Daniel also discuss the rapid growth of Oklahoma City, why exposure changes lives, how families need community around them, and why leadership rooted in love creates stronger cities.

This conversation feels deeply Oklahoma City.

Thoughtful. Honest. Hopeful.

It’s a reminder that the future of a city is shaped not only by development projects and arenas, but by people quietly choosing integrity, generosity, and stewardship every single day.
