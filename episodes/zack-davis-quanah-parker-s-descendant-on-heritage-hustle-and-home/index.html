Zack Davis: Quanah Parker's Descendant on Heritage, Hustle, and Home

VOICES of OKC Wed, 15 Jul 2026 20:29:23 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Zack Davis — OKC realtor, investor, and host of the Keep Going Podcast — is a direct descendant of Quanah Parker. He shares the story of his first Comanche gourd dance, two years after losing his grandfather, and the $300-paycheck low point that nearly ended his real estate career before it started.

What this conversation explores:
- Growing up in Del City and Moore with a single mom and "a lot of alone time"
- Basketball as first identity and first community
- Comanche and Caddo heritage, and descent from Quanah Parker
- His first gourd dance — the eagle wing, the elders, his grandfather's medicine
- The garage moment: $300 every two weeks and best friends who said keep going
- People-over-money selling, calmness, and getting 1% better

About the Guest:
Zack Davis is a realtor with Copper Creek Real Estate and a real estate investor with Local Guy Buys Houses in Oklahoma City, working in real estate since 2017 and licensed since 2019. He hosts the Keep Going Podcast and is Comanche and Caddo, a direct descendant of Quanah Parker.
