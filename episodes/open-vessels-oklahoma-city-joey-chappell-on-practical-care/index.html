Open Vessels Oklahoma City: Joey Chappell on Practical Care

VOICES of OKC Thu, 01 Oct 2026 13:17:59 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Joey Chappell of Open Vessels Oklahoma City talks about practical care—and why helping a neighbor cannot depend on being thanked.

A home repair may look like a small job from the street. For the person living there, it can change daily life. Joey Chappell joins his brother Jed to talk about the people behind those needs and the work of Open Vessels.

Their conversation moves from family and personal responsibility to recovery, faith and the limits of a small nonprofit. Joey describes what it takes to keep showing up, including the moments when gratitude arrives much later than expected.

Content note: Discussion of addiction, incarceration and family hardship; non-graphic accounts of neighbors experiencing disability and financial need.

What you’ll hear
- Taking responsibility without losing the possibility of change
- What recovery volunteers taught Joey about serving without applause
- Faith expressed through repairs, relationships and practical care
- The needs a small nonprofit can meet—and the resources it still needs
