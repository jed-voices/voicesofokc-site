Episode

Ariel Moore on Faith, Recovery, and the Freedom to Be Known

VOICES of OKC Sun, 25 Oct 2026 16:40:48 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Ariel Moore, author of Divine Authenticity, joins Jed Chappell to reflect on addiction, motherhood, faith, and rebuilding a life. She describes the practical support that gave her room to change, the spiritual life she developed, and the freedom of bringing her whole story into the open. Together, they consider how relationships, opportunity, and a safe place to belong can change what someone sees as possible.

Content note: Addiction, attempted suicide, family separation, violence, and trauma.

Ariel Moore remembers what it was like to need help while fearing the consequences of asking for it. Her conversation with Jed Chappell follows the path from that uncertainty toward recovery, reunification, meaningful work, and a life she now shares openly.

In Divine Authenticity, she writes about the experiences and beliefs that shaped that change. On this episode, she explains why honest writing required more than recounting events. It meant making room for the parts of her story she worried people might not understand.

The conversation explores:
- Why safe housing, relationships, and practical support matter alongside a person's desire to change.
- How being seen and loved can open possibilities that once seemed distant.
- Ariel's account of faith, prayer, and the spiritual practices central to her life.
- Vulnerability, forgiveness, and the difference between hiding a past and learning to speak about it.
