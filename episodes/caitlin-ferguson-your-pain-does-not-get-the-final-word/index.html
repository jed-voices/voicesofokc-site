Caitlin Ferguson - Your Pain Does Not Get the Final Word

VOICES of OKC Wed, 05 Aug 2026 06:20:24 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Caitlin Ferguson spent years trying to understand where she belonged.

Growing up biracial, navigating different faith traditions, and later surviving sexual assault, addiction, and mental-health crisis, Caitlin often felt unseen and uncertain about her identity.

In this honest conversation, Caitlin shares how faith, healthy relationships, and serving others helped her begin rebuilding her life. She also describes the encounter during a City Center mission trip that clarified her calling: a woman looked at her and said, “No one sees me.”

Today, Caitlin is preparing to create a ministry where women experiencing trauma and instability can find safety, dignity, practical support, and a place to start again.

This episode is a powerful reminder that pain does not have to define a life—and that it is never too late to keep going.

Content note: This episode discusses sexual assault, addiction, and mental-health crisis.
