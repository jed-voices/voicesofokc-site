Episode

Kristina Mills on grief, laughter and life after Survivor

VOICES of OKC Thu, 24 Sep 2026 10:45:00 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Kristina Mills, an educator and Survivor contestant, talks about living with grief while making room for laughter, family and new challenges. She joins Jed Chappell to explore what supportive friendship can look like, how the game changed her sense of identity, and why moving forward does not mean leaving the person you miss behind.

What this conversation explores
- Laughter and ordinary family life after loss
- How to share good news with a grieving friend
- Meals, practical help and messages that need no reply
- Identity beyond being a wife, mother, daughter and educator
- An unexpected moment on a Survivor challenge beam

Kristina Mills is an educator and Survivor season 49 contestant. She describes herself as a wife and mother and discusses speaking to school audiences about her experiences.
