Seeing People Beyond Their Labels | Janelle Nightingale

VOICES of OKC Wed, 18 Nov 2026 10:00:00 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

A tattoo can tell you something about a person's past. It cannot tell you everything about who they are becoming.

Author and documentary photographer Janelle Nightingale joins Jed Chappell to talk about the stories behind Resonance: The Quiet Revolution of Compassion. From Oklahoma tattoo artists helping people cover symbols of hate to a longtime volunteer who keeps showing up, Janelle looks at what becomes possible when we stay curious about one another.

The conversation moves through recovery, community, her relationships in Uganda, and the difference between listening to understand and preparing an answer. It also leaves room for an important boundary: compassion does not require agreement or renewed contact with someone who has harmed you.
