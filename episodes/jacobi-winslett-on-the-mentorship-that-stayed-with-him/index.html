Jacobi Winslett on the Mentorship That Stayed With Him

VOICES of OKC Fri, 16 Oct 2026 08:23:46 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Jacobi Winslett reflects on Oklahoma City mentorship and the adults who helped him see more possibilities for his life. From an after-school program at Novo Ministries to changes in education and work, he considers what that early attention means now. His conversation with Jed Chappell connects leading by example at TimberCraft Homes with a renewed desire to give younger people his time.

Content note: Discussion of economic hardship and stressful experiences in law enforcement.

What this conversation explores

  • The meaning of early mentorship can become clearer years after the program ends.
  • Doing the work firsthand shapes what a leader asks of other people.
  • Gratitude can become a practical commitment to give someone else time and attention.

About the guest

Jacobi Winslett is Vice President of Sales & Marketing at TimberCraft Homes, confirmed on its team page. He grew up in Oklahoma City and attended Millwood. In this conversation, he describes participating in Novo Ministries before volunteering and working there as a teenager, changing direction in school and work, and deciding to mentor younger people. Current role: https://timbercraft.co/our-team/ (checked October 12, 2026). Other biographical context is drawn from the interview.
