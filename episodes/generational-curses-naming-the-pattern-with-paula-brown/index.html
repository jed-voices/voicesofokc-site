Generational Curses: Naming the Pattern with Paula Brown

VOICES of OKC Thu, 17 Sep 2026 08:38:45 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Dr. Paula Brown, an author and Oklahoma City parent, examines generational curses and the family patterns people can mistake for normal. Drawing from her Trinidad and Tobago roots, motherhood, faith, education, and lived experience, she and Jed Chappell consider how honest recognition, safe adults, counseling, and practical choices can help interrupt a cycle without reducing a family to its hardest chapter.

What this conversation explores

- The difference between inherited biology and repeated family behavior
- Why the “ham in the pan” story explains unquestioned habits so well
- Addiction, secrecy, and the importance of naming patterns without shame
- Safe, trustworthy relationships for young people
- Education, mental-health support, reentry, and second chances
- The sacrifices Paula made while earning degrees and raising four children

Guest bio

Dr. Paula Brown is the author of *Generational Curses and How They Can Be Broken*. In the conversation, she describes growing up in Trinidad and Tobago, raising four children through military moves and deployments, and earning associate’s, bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees while caring for her family. Her comments connect family healing with faith, education, counseling, trustworthy relationships, and civic responsibility in Oklahoma City.
