The Pilot Who Saw Freedom Behind Prison Walls with Blake Raney

VOICES of OKC Wed, 08 Jul 2026 18:37:54 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

Blake Raney expected aviation to be about flying. Over time, it became a way to serve people who often feel unseen.

Blake, founder of SPAIRROW, joins Jed Chappell for a conversation about aviation, calling, business as service, prison ministry, fatherhood, and building a foundation before the storm.

He shares how an unexpected appointment to Oklahoma’s aerospace and aeronautics work challenged his confidence, how a business relationship changed the way he thought about work, and what he saw the first time he walked into a prison. The conversation also moves into the story of Blake and Anna’s daughter Kennedy, her heart surgery, and the moments that reminded their family they were not alone.

Content note: This conversation includes discussion of prison, addiction, abusive or absent fathers, infertility, and infant heart surgery. Nothing is graphic.

WHAT YOU'LL HEAR
- How Blake’s lifelong pull toward aviation became a business and calling
- Why SPAIRROW was built around helping people feel seen and valued
- What Blake saw in prison that changed his understanding of freedom
- Why fatherhood, accountability, and leadership matter for breaking cycles
- The story of Kennedy’s surgery and why Blake says to build your foundation before the storm

ABOUT BLAKE RANEY
Blake Raney is the founder of SPAIRROW, an Oklahoma City-based aviation company focused on aircraft management, brokerage, maintenance, and pilot services. He also serves as the District 5 commissioner on the Oklahoma Aerospace and Aeronautics Commission. Blake and his wife, Anna, live in Oklahoma City with their daughter, Kennedy.
