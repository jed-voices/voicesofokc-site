Your Beginning Doesn’t Decide Your Future with Joshua Banks

VOICES of OKC Wed, 11 Nov 2026 10:00:00 -0500 Show notes from Podbean
YouTube Apple Spotify Podbean
Show notes

A difficult beginning is part of a story. It does not have to decide its ending.

Joshua Banks joins Jed Chappell to talk about the teachers who helped him catch up, building a business, and showing young people that more is possible. Their conversation connects education, entrepreneurship, faith, communication, and the importance of feeling understood.

Joshua shares forgiveness as part of his own experience and reflects on the people who kept showing up when he needed support.
